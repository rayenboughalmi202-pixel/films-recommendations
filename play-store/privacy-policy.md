# Privacy Policy — Yami_Films

_Last updated: 31 October 2026_

Yami_Films is a personal movie-recommendation app. This policy explains, simply, what data the app uses.

## What the app collects

**Nothing is collected by the developer.** Yami_Films has:

- No user accounts
- No analytics
- No advertising
- No tracking
- No developer server — there is nowhere for your data to be sent to the developer

## What stays on your phone

- Your **TMDB API key**, if you enter one, is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. It is used only to talk to TMDB, and you can erase it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.
- Your searches are not stored or shared by the app.

## What is sent to TMDB

When you use online search, the movie title you type is sent to **The Movie Database (TMDB)** over the internet, so TMDB can return movie results, posters and details. That request is covered by TMDB's own privacy policy: https://www.themoviedb.org/privacy-policy

No other third party receives anything from the app.

## Children

The app is a general-audience movie information app. It does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address as this page, with a new “last updated” date.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: use the developer contact email shown on the Yami_Films Google Play listing.
