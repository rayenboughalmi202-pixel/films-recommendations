## Full description
🎬 **Yami_Films recommends movies similar to the ones you already love.**

Type the title of a movie you enjoyed, tap the right result, and Yami_Films shows you the 5 most similar movies — with posters, ratings, year, genres and a similarity score.

**How it works**
Yami_Films is powered by a real content-based recommendation algorithm that runs on your phone:
• Your search goes to The Movie Database (TMDB) to find the movie, its genres, overview and keywords
• A pool of popular and top-rated movies is fetched
• TF-IDF turns each movie's text into numbers, and cosine similarity ranks the most similar ones — on your device, not in the cloud

**Features**
• Search almost any movie online (free TMDB API key required, pasted once inside the app)
• Movie posters, ratings, years and genres
• A built-in offline list of well-known movies, so the app still works with no connection
• No account, no ads, no tracking

**Good to know**
• Online mode needs your own free TMDB API key: create an account on themoviedb.org, copy your key from Settings → API, and paste it in the app under “TMDB key”.
• Your key is stored only on your phone and is never sent anywhere except to TMDB.

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
Movie data and posters are provided by TMDB (themoviedb.org).
